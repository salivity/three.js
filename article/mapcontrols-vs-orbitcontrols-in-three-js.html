MapControls vs OrbitControls in Three.js

In Three.js, MapControls is an extension of OrbitControls specifically designed to replicate the navigation dynamics of digital mapping services like Google Maps. While both control sets allow users to pan, rotate, and zoom around a central target in a 3D scene, MapControls alters default mouse bindings, touch interactions, and panning planes to make traversing flat terrain intuitive and stable.

Ground-Plane Panning vs. Screen-Space Panning

The primary mechanical difference when navigating flat terrain lies in the screenSpacePanning property.

  • OrbitControls sets screenSpacePanning to true by default. When you pan with the camera tilted toward the ground, the camera moves strictly up, down, left, or right relative to the camera viewport. Panning upward pulls the camera diagonally into the air, while panning downward pushes it toward or below the terrain surface.
  • MapControls forces screenSpacePanning to false by default. Panning is locked to the world coordinate ground plane (typically the XZ plane). Dragging the view slides the camera parallel to the terrain without changing its altitude or vertical distance relative to the ground, keeping the horizon and ground perspective consistent.

Mouse Button Bindings

MapControls reverses the default primary mouse actions of OrbitControls to prioritize translation over rotation:

  • Left Mouse Button: In OrbitControls, left-clicking rotates (orbits) the camera around the target point. In MapControls, left-clicking pans the camera across the terrain surface.
  • Right Mouse Button: In OrbitControls, right-clicking pans the camera. In MapControls, right-clicking (or left-clicking while holding Ctrl) orbits the camera, allowing the user to tilt the pitch or rotate the compass bearing of the map.
  • Scroll Wheel: Both controllers use the scroll wheel to zoom (dolly) in and out toward the cursor's focus area or target.

Touch Interactions on Mobile

The touch defaults in MapControls are tailored for flat navigation:

  • One-Finger Drag: Configured to pan across the terrain plane instead of rotating the view.
  • Two-Finger Gesture: Handles simultaneous pinch-to-zoom and two-finger rotation, closely matching native touchscreen map applications.

By locking movement to the horizontal surface and prioritizing panning with the primary input, MapControls prevents accidental disorientation and ensures flat planes remain stable beneath the user's viewport.
